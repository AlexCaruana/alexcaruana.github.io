It's been a couple of weeks since I returned from [ECCB 2026](https://eccb26leiden.eu/) in Leiden, where I had the wonderful opportunity to present ""The State of reproducibility within Conservation Science impact evaluations"", which draws from preliminary results from my second thesis chapter. The reception from the audience was fantastic, and I had some great conversations with conservation scientists and practitioners who had run into the same computational reproducibility challenges in their own work. As always at [SCB-IEWG events](https://scb-impact.org/eccb-2026/), the social gatherings were just as valuable as the sessions themselves, sparking some brilliant discussions with like-minded researchers in the field.
